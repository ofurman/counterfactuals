[official GitHub logo package](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip), linked
from the [GitHub Brand Toolkit](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo).

The mark identifies the repository destination, not institutional sponsorship.
It retains its original black artwork, square viewport, and internal clear space.
The QR uses high error correction, a four-module outer quiet zone, and a cleared
center behind the embedded image. Both QR and logo are bundled for offline print.
