# NeurIPS Poster Inspection Notes

These notes describe the downloaded originals in `assets/`. Lines labelled **Observation** report
visible features of the source image. Lines labelled **Inference** state how that evidence may
inform the CEL poster; they are design judgments, not claims made by the cited paper.

## OpenXAI

- **Observation [openxai-2022] — hierarchy:** A large red serif title spans the page; red section
  bars divide a three-column body, with `TL;DR` and `Contributions` at the upper left.
- **Observation [openxai-2022] — reading order:** The columns progress from motivation and scope,
  through API examples and metric descriptions, to leaderboards and experiments.
- **Observation [openxai-2022] — density/type:** Long bullet blocks and several small tables make
  the lower half text-heavy; body type is sans serif and code snippets use a monospace face.
- **Observation [openxai-2022] — palette:** Crimson navigation bars and a small blue chart palette
  sit on a white ground.
- **Observation [openxai-2022] — QR:** One red QR appears at lower right under the explicit label
  `Project website`.
- **Observation [openxai-2022] — strongest pattern:** The top-left summary and labelled project QR
  make the contribution and destination immediately identifiable.
- **Observation [openxai-2022] — failure mode:** The middle column contains paragraph-length metric
  definitions and the right column includes two small tables that are difficult at overview scale.
- **Inference [openxai-2022]:** CEL can reuse the contribution-first opening and one labelled QR,
  while replacing API prose and tables with a benchmark pipeline and larger trade-off graphics.

- **Observation [adbench-2022] — QR:** No QR code is visible on the downloaded poster.
